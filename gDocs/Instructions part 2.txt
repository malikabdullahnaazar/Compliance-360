1. Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Hospice Chart Audit Platform
Scope: Admission + Recertification
Frameworks: CMS + CHAP (HHSC overlay, Texas-first)
________________________________________
Objective
Enable hospice agencies to proactively identify and correct admission and recertification documentation deficiencies aligned with CMS CoPs, CHAP standards, and Texas HHSC expectations—before survey.
________________________________________
In-Scope (MVP)
Audit Types
•	Admission Audit
•	Recertification Audit (90-day, 60-day, subsequent periods)
Frameworks
•	CMS Hospice CoPs
•	CHAP Hospice Standards
•	HHSC Hospice rules (overlay tags + reporting)
Outputs
•	Deficiency list with:
o	Severity
o	Framework citations
o	Evidence links
•	Correction guidance (safe, non-fabricating)
•	Survey-ready PDF + CAPA summary
________________________________________
Out of Scope (MVP)
•	Routine visit audits
•	Bereavement-only audits
•	EMR write-back
•	Auto-generated clinical narratives
________________________________________
User Roles
•	QA/Compliance: runs audits, assigns corrections
•	Clinical Leadership: reviews trends, signs off
•	Clinicians: resolve assigned correction tasks
________________________________________
Success Metrics
•	% of admission charts passing without critical deficiencies
•	Reduction in repeat deficiencies at recert
•	Time to audit completion
•	Time to correction closure
________________________________________
2. Admission + Recert Common Requirements Library (CRL)
Below are 40 high-risk, survey-relevant checks.
Each check is one rule, mapped to CMS, CHAP, and HHSC.
________________________________________
A. Admission – Election & Eligibility (Checks 1–15)
Election & Rights
1.	Hospice election statement present
2.	Election includes all required elements
3.	Patient/representative signature present
4.	Election effective date documented
5.	Attending physician selection documented
6.	Patient rights acknowledgment present
7.	Notice of hospice election provided timely
Certification of Terminal Illness (CTI)
8.	Initial CTI present
9.	CTI signed by appropriate physician
10.	CTI dated timely relative to admission
11.	Diagnosis on CTI matches medical record
12.	Prognosis timeframe documented (≤ 6 months)
Eligibility Support
13.	Clinical narrative supports terminal prognosis
14.	Supporting documentation present (LCD indicators / decline)
15.	No contradictory documentation (e.g., “improving,” curative goals)
________________________________________
B. Admission – Assessments & Plan of Care (Checks 16–25)
16.	Initial RN assessment completed timely
17.	Comprehensive assessment completed within required timeframe
18.	Pain assessment documented
19.	Symptom assessment documented (dyspnea, anxiety, etc.)
20.	Psychosocial assessment present
21.	Spiritual assessment present or declined
22.	Initial Plan of Care created
23.	POC addresses assessed problems
24.	POC includes measurable goals
25.	Visit frequencies align with POC
________________________________________
C. Admission – Orders & Medications (Checks 26–30)
26.	Admission orders present
27.	Orders signed and dated appropriately
28.	Verbal/telephone orders meet requirements
29.	Medication profile present
30.	Allergies documented
________________________________________
D. Recertification – Timing & F2F (Checks 31–36)
31.	Recertification CTI present for period
32.	Recert CTI signed by physician
33.	Recert CTI completed timely
34.	Face-to-Face encounter completed when required
35.	F2F performed by eligible practitioner
36.	F2F attestation present and complete
________________________________________
E. Recertification – Ongoing Eligibility (Checks 37–40)
37.	Recert narrative supports continued decline
38.	Decline indicators consistent across disciplines
39.	IDG reviewed and documented recert decision
40.	POC updated to reflect current status
________________________________________
HHSC Overlay (Texas)
Each applicable finding gets tagged if it implicates:
•	Patient rights
•	Physician involvement
•	Timeliness
•	Documentation completeness
This enables state survey–specific reporting without duplicating rules.
________________________________________
3. Audit Logic (How the system evaluates charts)
Timing Engine
•	Admission date = anchor
•	Recert periods calculated automatically
•	F2F windows enforced (days relative to benefit period)
Evidence Matching
•	Documents classified (CTI, election, F2F, POC, assessments)
•	Key fields extracted:
o	Dates
o	Signatures
o	Author role
o	Diagnosis
•	Conflicts flagged (e.g., decline vs improvement language)
________________________________________
Severity Model
•	Critical: likely CMS/CHAP condition-level finding
•	Major: standard-level deficiency
•	Moderate: correctable but risky
•	Minor: documentation cleanup
•	Advisory: best practice
________________________________________
4. Correction Guidance Model (Key differentiator)
Each failed check generates a Correction Card:
Correction Card Includes:
•	What is missing or noncompliant
•	Why it matters (survey risk)
•	Applicable CMS / CHAP / HHSC reference
•	Who must correct it (RN, MD, IDG, etc.)
•	Where correction belongs
•	Allowed correction method:
o	Addendum
o	Late entry
o	New documentation
•	Required attestations
•	Prompted questions (to prevent fabrication)
⚠️ The system never invents clinical facts — it guides staff on what must be clarified or documented.
